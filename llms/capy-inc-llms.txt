# Capy Inc. (Lemin Captcha)

> Capy Inc. operates Lemin Captcha, a gamified bot-prevention and CAPTCHA service that replaces image-selection challenges with brandable puzzle games. Websites embed a client-side widget and verify the visitor's encrypted answer server-side against the Lemin verification API. Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from Lemin's public developer documentation; the provider does not publish an llms.txt.

## APIs

- [Lemin Captcha Verification API](https://help.leminnow.com/knowledge/developers-guide): POST https://api.leminnow.com/captcha/v1/cropped/validate — verify a visitor's encrypted cropped-captcha answer using your account private_key.
